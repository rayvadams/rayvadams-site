About

I am an applied mathematician and an independent researcher in the calculus of variations and optimal control. After 27 years in fixed-income finance at a buy-side firm, I have returned to research at Worcester Polytechnic Institute, where I earned my M.S. in Applied Mathematics, with my thesis advisor, Konstantin A. Lurie, and Dr. William C. Sanguinet, who completed his Ph.D. with Lurie some years after me.

My current work is on the optimal control of composite materials: how to arrange isotropic materials, down to their microstructure, so that a body does a job as well as it possibly can. My interests include the calculus of variations, optimal control, homogenization, and Γ‑convergence. Most recently, I have been studying Lepage–Dedecker formulations of the variational bicomplex and Agrachev's geometric control theory.
